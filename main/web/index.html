Hardware baseline

ESP32-S3 N32R16V · 8 × SC09 + 1 × SC15 · brushed tracks · 3S battery · SPI LCD · UART media.

Battery ADC uses 100k / 33k plus 100 nF at the ADC pin. Do not connect the pack directly to any logic or servo rail. An independent actuator power cutoff is still required.
